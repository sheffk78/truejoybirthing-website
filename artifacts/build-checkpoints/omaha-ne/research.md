# Omaha, NE — BUILD Research Checkpoint
Date: 2026-08-25 · Slug: omaha-ne · Tier: large metro (~486k)

## Doula market
- Bornbir: Omaha birth doula cost **$800–$2,500** (avg range $800–$2,000); postpartum doulas $25–$30/hr.
- Providers identified:
  - **Omaha Birth & Babies** — agency, 4611 S 96th St Ste 169; certified birth+postpartum doulas, overnight care; 4.9★/116 reviews. https://omahabirthandbabies.com/
  - **The Omaha Baby Nest** — full-service doula agency; co-owner Mariah (DONA birth doula, ProDoula certified); birth+postpartum, breastfeeding support, childbirth ed. https://www.theomahababynest.com/
  - **Mother Knows Birth** — birth doula, VBAC/TOLAC support; serves within 1 hr of Omaha incl. Gretna, Elkhorn, Bellevue, Papillion, Lincoln. https://www.motherknowsbirthdoula.com/
  - **Downtown Doula of Omaha** — certified birth+postpartum doulas, 1029 S 30th Ave; 5★/11 reviews.
  - **Doulas at Essentials (Jenn Barnes)** — certified birth+postpartum doula, lactation background; Omaha Better Birth Project grant partner.
  - **Prairieland Birth Works** — certified birth doula, 10+ yrs experience. https://www.prairielandbirthworks.com/

## Hospitals (labor & delivery)
- **Methodist Women's Hospital** — dedicated women's hospital; L&D unit publishes a birth plan worksheet; CMS Birthing-Friendly (Methodist Hospital also Birthing-Friendly). https://bestcare.org/locations/methodist-womens-hospital-labor-and-delivery-unit
- **CHI Health Maternity Center at Bergan Mercy** — 7500 Mercy Rd, Omaha NE 68124; Birthing-Friendly. https://www.chihealth.com/services/maternity/our-centers
- **Nebraska Medicine / The Nebraska Medical Center** — 42nd & Emile; high-risk maternity care. https://www.nebraskamed.com/womens-health/pregnancy-birth
- Also Birthing-Friendly per minimalistmama roundup: CHI Immanuel, CHI Lakeside.

## Birth centers
- Nebraska's only freestanding birth center announced closure (KETV, after ~500 births) → **no active freestanding birth center in Omaha**; hospital-based CNM care is the midwifery path.

## State stats & coverage
- Cesarean rate (all births): ~31.6% (CDC/NCHS 2023).
- Maternal mortality: ~23–26 per 100k live births (CDC NVSS 2019–2023 window).
- Home birth rate: ~1.5% of Nebraska births.
- Medicaid: Nebraska Heritage Health (managed care) covers births incl. in-network freestanding birth center care; **no statewide Medicaid doula benefit implemented**. Private doula coverage rare but growing.
