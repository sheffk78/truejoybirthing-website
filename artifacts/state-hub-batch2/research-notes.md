## Medicaid doula coverage status (Pn3 2025 roadmap)
- AR: YES (one of 26 states; Act 1210 of 2021 pilot → Healthy Moms, Healthy Babies Act 2025
  made doula reimbursement statute; UAMS runs doula scholarship/training)
- DE: implementation in progress (no live reimbursement confirmed in roadmap)
- HI: NO (no bills last year)
- IA: NO (no bills last year)
- KS: YES (implemented July 1, 2024 under SB 315/KanCare; ~$1,300 base per birth)
- LA: YES (HB 272 of 2023 requires Medicaid coverage of maternity support services/doulas)
- ME: in progress — LD 1523 resolve directs DHHS rate determination by Jan 1 2026
- MS: NO (roadmap: no coverage, no training support; HB 1725 2024 died in Senate; CHAMPS
  Kellogg-funded pilot in Jackson)
- MT: authorization exists (SB 319 licensed doulas + Medicaid option) but implementation
  paused per bozeman page; roadmap classifies as not-yet
- ND: NO (H.B. 1012 enacted — interim study of maternal health incl. doula reimbursement)
- NH: in process — Momnibus 2023 appropriated doula Medicaid; as of Sep 2025 no SPA approved
- SD: YES (became reimbursement state in 2025; up to $1,800 total ≈17 hours + L&D)
- VT: in process — S.53 enacted; reimbursement required by July 1, 2026; $25k FY26 for cert
- WV: NO (training grant program yes; SB 40/SB 292/HB 2455 failed)
- WY: NO (no bills)

## More verified facts (batch 2)
- DE birth center: The Birth Center Holistic Women's Healthcare (Newark DE, midwifery-led,
  birthing tubs; operating since 1990s — moved location, approved by Delaware Health Resources
  Board 2017). Delaware has free-standing birthing center regs (16 DE Admin Code 3365).
- IA midwife licensing: Iowa Code chapter 148I (created 2016, amended by HF 265 of 2023);
  Board of Nursing adopted CPM licensure rules (ARC 8114C) — Iowa NOW licenses CPMs.
  CNMs licensed as ARPNs. Iowa Medicaid doula: NOT covered (only demo pending; SF 132?? —
  actually the Pn3 2025 roadmap says Iowa had NO doula bills last year — treat as no coverage).
- Hawaii: midwives = CNMs licensed as APRNs (Kaiser on Oahu/Maui/Hawaii Island, Tripler);
  home-birth midwives (CPM/DEM) recognized — Hawaii licensed "midwives" via Act 19?? — the
  Midwives Alliance page implies both CNM and direct-entry midwives practice legally.
  Hawaii birth culture: highest home-birth rate in country (~10%+ of births out of hospital,
  among the nation's highest), Kapiolani = only specialty women's/children's hospital,
  >6,200 births/yr, largest NICU (1,000+/yr). Maui: OB practice closures + 2023 wildfire;
  Malama I Ke Ola (Community Clinic of Maui) expanded prenatal capacity. Molokai General
  L&D status fragile (sometimes without surgeon coverage) — phrase carefully.
