## MedicaidNote
California SB-509: Medi-Cal covers doula services statewide since Jan 2023. Reimbursement ~$1,587 per birth package (prenatal + labor + postpartum). Doulas enroll as Medi-Cal providers via DHCS and bill through PAVE.

## InsuranceNote
SB 332 requires most commercial / Covered CA / large-group plans to cover licensed midwife and birth center care; out-of-network doula reimbursement varies.
